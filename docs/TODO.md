# User requests and bug reports

T19 (2026-10-16, on `wt/library-1000`, not merged): the user asked to scour the web for more MusicXML and MIDI and to grow the library by at least 1,000 scores, for a private app ("we do not have to worry about copyright"). Judgement: PDMX, the MuseScore archive of about 254,000 scores that uploaders declared public domain or CC0 and that PDMX screened for licence conflicts, is the only source of that size for piano solos; it is the one source added this round. The scores pass the library's own gates (two staves with notes on both, zero lint law errors at medium and large, no unplayable slice, the MusicXML schema and the validator) and the scope stays classical, folk and religious (MuseScore's own tags; no pop). Copyright: the user chose the broad reading, so these scores are not held to the public-repo author-date rule (author died 1955 or earlier); they are taken on the uploader's declared licence, which the notes say. Result: 1,429 archive pieces, 1,682 in the library; 1,102 of the 1,429 have no composer in the archive and read "Unknown". Storage: scores gzip-compressed (`.musicxml.gz`), the library list fetched before the app renders (a bundled list was 216 KB gzip, over the 150 KB budget), the repo budget raised from 60 MB to 100 MB and the generated records given their own limits. Level split is the frozen heuristic's (8, 56, 669, 564, 3 and 30 placed at 6); the library now leans to levels 3 and 4 and needs a judgement. Follow-ups from the user the same day: at least 1,000 more at level 6 (judgement: the heuristic has no level 6 and the archive holds about 235 candidates at most; shipped 30 by a stated rule, then, at the user's choice, 99 more with the span and lint gates relaxed for level 6 under a ceiling of 40 law errors per 1,000 notes: 129 archive pieces at level 6, 179 in the library, still far from 1,000; the next lever is an advanced Mutopia lane) and dedupe (done by note content: 156 archive copies removed). A scope review also removed 59 pop, game and Nazi-era pieces the genre tags let through. Pipeline and counts: `content/tools/README.md`, "MuseScore archive scores".
