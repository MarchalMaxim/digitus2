Chosen: K 5, slow 0.5, W 10, not by train gen alone. A plain high hop weight gains up to 0.6 more train gen, but it also makes a slow same-finger shift over a wide leap expensive, so the solver stretches beyond the practical span instead (library lint errors 27.85 → 37.44 per 1000 at W 100); the within-a-fourth rule keeps wide slow leaps cheap and reaches the textbook plateau (0.842 to 0.850) at W 10. Among K 4, 5 and 7 at W 10 the differences are within 0.06 train gen; K 5 also fingers the left-hand C major arpeggio 5 3 2 1 3 2 1 without the template bonus. Further gains are left to M3's fitted weights.
