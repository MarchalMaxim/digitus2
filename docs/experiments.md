### Law errors of the learned sets on validation (2026-10-14, M3 Task 7)

Question (task brief): Task 5 measured 3.91 lint errors per 1000 test notes for both learned sets against 3.81 for the rules, and the hardening invariant says no law error may occur where a legal fingering exists; do the learned sets break it? Measured on the 20 validation pieces with an exact dynamic programme over every chord pair and held pair (chord order kept) that gives each hand's minimum number of law errors (L01 to L05), checked by linting its optimum (equal to the minimum on every hand). Per-hand table in `docs/research/hard-errors-m3.md`.

Answer: no. The 31 hands that have a fully legal fingering get one from all three sets. All law errors are in the 9 hands that cannot be fingered legally (exact minimum 94 errors in all, 10.52 per 1000): parncutt 97, learned-pig 97, learned-open 98. The excess is in three hands and shared by every set: 031 R (60 against 59), 038 R (5 against 4) and 038 L (rules and pig 7, learned-open 8, against 6). learned-open's extra error is in 038 L, where it puts Eb3 and the C#2 that sounds under it on one finger. These errors are not forced (the minimum is lower) but they are not where a legal fingering exists either: they come from the law-domain pass, which drops the oldest held key's law when the laws cannot all hold, and that drop is not always the one that breaks the fewest laws. Raising the loop budgets (32 rounds) changes nothing; switching the pass off makes it worse (109 and 104). Left as a finding for the solver's law pass (a minimum-violation repair for hands without a legal fingering); lint is right. The consistency pass (H4b) changes none of these counts.

Change (`packages/engine/src/learn/calibration.ts`): soft labels. A note's target is the share of its annotators who use each finger (`ConfidenceSample.target`, built by `confidenceSample`); the likelihood is the cross-entropy against that share (each annotator a sample of weight 1 over the note's annotators; an annotator whose finger the solver prices at a hard-rule penalty, margin 500 or more, is left out and the others keep their share); a bucket's accuracy is the mean share of annotators who use the chosen finger.
