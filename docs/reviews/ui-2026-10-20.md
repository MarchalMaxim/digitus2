# UI review, 2026-10-20: the hand switch (issue #79)

A qualitative UI review (verification spec 3.10) of one change: the hand switch of [issue #79](https://github.com/MarchalMaxim/digitus2/issues/79), described in `docs/research/hand-switch-performance.md`. The reviewer edited no product code. Nothing else in the app was reviewed.

Result: no blockers. A switch is immediate at full speed. The resting look equals the reduced-motion look pixel for pixel and matches the old look. Print, playback and scrolling are right, and the one-red rule holds. One new glitch (H1) appears on quick toggles.

Severity: blocker (a beginner cannot proceed), major (a beginner will be confused or stuck), minor (friction or a visible glitch), nit (polish).

## Scope and method

- The production build in `apps/web/dist`, built after the last source edit, so it includes the per-page bar measurement for click-to-seek. Served with `vite preview` on port 5541 and driven by Playwright's Chromium (headless, Windows 11), service workers blocked, `?audio=silent`.
- 1280 x 900 at device pixel ratio 1, and 360 x 740 at ratio 3 (resting look) or 2 (Scherzo scrolling, touch enabled). Light and dark.
- Pieces: Twinkle, Mozart K.545, Kuhlau's Rondo, Chopin's Scherzo No. 2 (780 bars; 32 pages at 1280, 121 at 360). "Left" means the left hand is practised and the right hand is dimmed.
- Frames of a switch were taken by pausing the score's animations at t ms after a real click, and by recording compositor frames (`Page.startScreencast`). Quick toggles were filmed at a tenth of the speed (`Animation.setPlaybackRate`). Blank pages were looked for in the compositor frames, never in `page.screenshot`, which forces a full render.
- Tone means the mean difference from the paper, from 0 to 255, inside the boxes of the notes or digits measured. A chromatic pixel is one whose channels differ by more than 24.
- The machine was shared with other sessions (CPU at 82 % during the first latency run). The latency table comes from a second run with 3 s between switches.

## 1. The dimmed hand at rest

The dimmed hand reads clearly as the other hand: the same 40 % gray in both themes, with the staff lines showing through it, as before ([light, phone](ui-2026-10-20/rest-light-phone.png), [dark, phone](ui-2026-10-20/rest-dark-phone.png)). At 1280 the crop of Kuhlau's second system is identical to the research's `light-1x-new.png` and `dark-1x-new.png` (0 pixels differ). Against the old crops it differs in 5,545 pixels in light (4.0 %) and 4,623 in dark (3.4 %), the same as the research reports.

The overlap seams are invisible at reading size. At 1280 I could not find them without zooming. On the phone crop they show only when it is magnified, as slightly darker stem ends inside the beams and stem edges along the noteheads ([phone crop at 9x](ui-2026-10-20/seams-phone-9x.png)). A teacher or a beginner would not see them.

The dimmed digits at rest are pure gray, with no colour fringe. The ink digits carry Windows' ClearType fringes, as all text does on this system; this is the baseline.

## 2. The switch

**Latency.** A switch feels immediate at full speed on all four pieces. On a CPU slowed 4x, it takes a fifth to a third of a second. Event Timing (input to next paint) of six real clicks, Left, Right, Both, Right, Left, Both, scrolled a third of the way down, 1280 x 900:

| Piece                           |   1x: range | 1x: median |  4x: range | 4x: median | Long tasks per switch at 4x |
| ------------------------------- | ----------: | ---------: | ---------: | ---------: | --------------------------: |
| Twinkle                         | all < 16 ms |          — |   16–32 ms |      24 ms |                        0 ms |
| Mozart                          |    32–56 ms |      32 ms | 152–264 ms |     160 ms |                   99–631 ms |
| Kuhlau                          |   56–104 ms |      64 ms | 192–368 ms |     224 ms |                  429–779 ms |
| Scherzo                         |    40–72 ms |      40 ms | 208–352 ms |     216 ms |                  205–573 ms |
| Scherzo, 360 x 740 (loaded run) |    32–96 ms |      48 ms | 168–328 ms |     200 ms |                           — |

These agree with the research's numbers after the change. The slowest switches are Left to Right and Right to Left, where both hands change, plus, on the Scherzo, the first switch to Left.

**The sweep.** The fade still reads as a sweep from left to right, bar by bar, in every system on screen ([frames at 0, 80, 160 and 240 ms](ui-2026-10-20/switch-frames-0-80-160-240ms.png), Kuhlau, Both to Left). One switch marks 245 elements in the 6 systems that meet the window, with 2 transitions each. A system below the window is already at 0.4 at 0 ms, with nothing swept. Tone of the right hand in the first and last bar of one system:

| t (ms)               |     0 |    80 |   140 |   160 |  240 |  300 |
| -------------------- | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ---: | ---: |
| first bar, noteheads | 164.7 |  87.6 |  77.2 |  76.6 | 76.6 | 76.6 |
| first bar, digits    |  59.6 |  27.5 |  23.3 |  17.9 | 17.9 | 17.9 |
| last bar, noteheads  | 177.5 | 177.5 | 177.5 | 154.2 | 90.9 | 85.4 |
| last bar, digits     |  61.1 |  61.1 |  61.1 |  51.4 | 28.0 | 20.7 |

The real-time screencast shows the same progression, and each probe moves only one way. Dark mode sweeps identically. A single switch always ends in the resting state of section 1.

**H1 (minor, new): a quick toggle flashes the digits of the hand coming back.** Left, then Right within about 100 ms: in the bars whose fade had not begun yet (still in their delay of up to 140 ms), the right hand's digits jump to pale at the click, stay pale for the rest of their delay, and then fade back up. Their notes stay in ink the whole time. Tone of those digits in the last bar of a system, filmed at a tenth of the speed: 57.5 (ink) before the click, 21.4 from 0 to 137 ms, 57.5 again at 190 ms. Left, then Both after 60 ms does the same. [Frame](ui-2026-10-20/quick-toggle-flash.png): bars 68 to 70 of the upper staff have pale digits on notes in ink. Cause: the `digit-back` animation starts from fill opacity 0.4 (`backwards`) on every digit brought back, including the ones that never dimmed. The old build had no such animation: its digits faded with the same `opacity` transition as the notes.

**H2 (nit, new): during its fade a digit is drawn as translucent text, then snaps to gray.** For the 160 ms of its fade, a dimmed digit is drawn at fill opacity, so it carries coloured fringes. At the last frame of the fade, the dimmed digits of the crop have 732 chromatic pixels among their 1,239 ink pixels, with a maximum chroma of 72 (light, 1280). Settled, they have none. When the fade ends, the digit settles into its group opacity and lightens a step: tone 22.8 to 18.7 in light, 20.3 to 17.6 in dark, with about 1,000 pixels of a system crop changing by more than 8. Compare [the last fade frame](ui-2026-10-20/digits-last-fade-frame.png) with [the digits at rest](ui-2026-10-20/digits-at-rest.png), at 4x. At real speed it is a faint tick at the end of each bar's fade, and only on Windows, where text gets subpixel smoothing.

**H3 (nit, new): quick toggles leave sweep marks behind.** A transition cancelled during its delay ends without `transitionend`, and nothing listens for `transitioncancel`. Its element keeps `.is-sweeping` and an inline `--sweep`. The marks have no visible effect at rest and are cleared at the next switch. The e2e check "no element is left marked" covers a single switch only. Two runs of each scenario on Kuhlau, ending 1 s after the last click:

| Scenario                    | Ends in the right state | px differing by > 8 from the reduced-motion state | Elements left marked |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------: | -------------------: |
| Left, 100 ms, Right         | yes                     |                                                 0 |                 9–15 |
| Left, 250 ms, Right         | yes                     |                                                 0 |                    0 |
| Left, 100, Right, 100, Both | yes                     |                                             18–61 |                28–42 |
| Left, 60 ms, Both           | yes                     |                                           138–179 |                23–26 |
| Right, 120 ms, Left         | yes                     |                                                 0 |                   14 |
| Left, 100 ms, Left again    | yes                     |                                                 0 |                    0 |
| Left, 30, Both, 30, Left    | yes                     |                                             20–34 |                    0 |
| Left, 100, Right, 100, Left | yes                     |                                              0–21 |                 0–22 |

"The right state" means the digit styles and dimmed counts equal those after a reduced-motion switch: no digit in a different tone and no bar left undimmed. The pixels that differ are scattered antialiasing, and I saw no difference at 4x zoom.

## 3. The Scherzo: one-hand playback and scrolling

**Playback across a page turn, Left hand.** The cursor stays smooth through the page turn, and no page shows blank, late or jumping: no frame of the recording has a blank stretch.

**Scrolling.** Page heights never change and the scroll position never jumps; `scrollHeight` stays at 33,303 px (1280) and 129,174 px (360). A blank stretch of more than 400 px means a page left undrawn (a page is 1,137 px tall at 1280; the gap between systems is about 40 px).

| Drag, 900 px per frame                         |      32 |                                    0 | drawn                    |

| End key (smooth scroll to the end)             |       — |  9, from 51 to 195 ms (whole window) | drawn at 275 ms          |
| Jump to 40 % or 80 % (`scrollTo`)              |       — |                     1 frame per jump | drawn by the next frame  |

**H4 (nit, new): a long jump shows one blank frame.** A page skipped by `content-visibility: auto` is drawn one frame after it lands in the window. A jump therefore shows a blank window for one frame ([frame](ui-2026-10-20/jump-blank-frame.png)), and the End key's smooth scroll shows it for about 150 ms. Wheels and drags of up to 900 px per frame never do. The 360 layout behaves the same (End: blank from 36 to 169 ms; a jump: one frame).

## 4. One red and the black-and-white brief

Exactly one red root held in every state I checked: rest (four theme and size combinations), the end of each switch (light and dark), playback across the page turn, the hover tint and seeks on pages 26 and 31 (1280) and 26 and 115 (360), and 24 samples taken while switching during playback. Apart from the accent, the only chromatic pixels are ClearType fringes on text, plus two transient cases: the fringes of H2 and the pink cursor of H5.

**Hover and seek far down.** At 1280 on page 26 of 32 (bar 646), the hover tint is a light ink box over the bar ([hover](ui-2026-10-20/hover-bar-646.png)). Its edges are on the bar lines (0.0 px left, 0.0 px right) and it reaches 21.6 px above and below the staves, the designed 0.75 staff height. A click there lands on bar 646, and its footprint sits on the same bar lines ([seek](ui-2026-10-20/seek-bar-646.png)). The per-page measurement is exact far down the piece:

| Where                      | Bar clicked or tapped | Bar landed on | Tint or footprint vs bar lines (left, right) | One red |
| -------------------------- | --------------------: | ------------: | -------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| 1280, page 26 of 32, click |                   646 |           646 | 0.0, 0.0 px                                  | yes     |
| 1280, page 31 of 32, click |                   761 |           761 | 0.0, 0.0 px                                  | yes     |
| 360, page 26 of 121, tap   |                   206 |           206 | 0.0, 0.0 px                                  | yes     |
| 360, page 115 of 121, tap  |                   739 |           739 | 0.0, 0.0 px                                  | yes     |

**H5 (minor, not from this change; fixable now): the red cursor can show pale right after a switch while playing.** In 5 of 12 switches made during playback of Kuhlau, the cursor's note sat inside a layer being dimmed or brought back. It inherited fill opacity 0.4 and drew pink ([crop at 4x](ui-2026-10-20/pale-cursor-after-switch.png)). For the hand left out, it stays pink until the cursor moves on. For the hand coming back, it stays pink until its fade ends, at most about 300 ms. The rules `.is-red .is-muted` and `.is-red.is-muted` cover a dimmed element inside the red, but not the red inside a dimmed ancestor. The old build has the same two rules with `opacity`, and a group opacity could not be undone from inside the group. With fill and stroke opacity it can now be undone in one rule.

## 5. Print

With Left selected, `print` media: every page has `content-visibility: visible` (5 of 5 on Kuhlau, 32 of 32 on the Scherzo). Every dimmed element (1,139 and 4,059) is at opacity, fill opacity and stroke opacity 1. `page.pdf()` gives 6 and 33 A4 pages. Rendered back with pdf.js, both hands are in full ink on every page I viewed: Kuhlau page 3 ([render](ui-2026-10-20/print-left-hand-selected.png)), and Scherzo pages 16 and 33, the last bars included. Scherzo page 32 was checked by its ink count only.

Not from this change: the first printed page carries only the header, because `break-inside: avoid` sends the first score page to page 2. The old build has the same rule.

## Ranked fixes

1. **H1, quick-toggle flash (minor, new; small enough to fix in this change).** Start `digit-back` only on a digit that was at rest when the switch came. A digit still marked by the previous switch should reverse its fill-opacity transition instead. Guard: after Left, then Right within 100 ms, no digit still marked by the first switch starts a `digit-back` animation, and the digits whose fade had not begun stay at fill opacity 1 through their delay.
2. **H5, pale red after a switch while playing (minor, older than this change).** Draw the accent whole wherever it sits, for example with `.score-sheet svg .is-red { fill-opacity: 1; stroke-opacity: 1 }`. Guard: while playing, a switch leaves every `.is-red` element at computed fill opacity 1.
3. **H3, marks left by cancelled transitions (nit).** On `transitioncancel`, settle the element when it has no running animation left (`el.getAnimations().length === 0`). Add the quick toggle to `hand-switch.spec.ts`, so that "no element left marked" also covers Left, then Right after 100 ms.
4. **H2, digit fringe and settle step during the fade (nit).** None needed now; it is the price of drawing no layers. If the owner notices it, try switching the digits at their bar's delay without a fade, and recheck the layer count.
5. **H4, one blank frame after a long jump (nit).** None; this is the cost of skipping the pages off screen, and the wheel, drags and playback are unaffected.
