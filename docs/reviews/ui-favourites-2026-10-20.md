# UI review: Favourites (issue #78), 2026-10-20

The heart on the score page and the Favourites shelf on the library, judged as a beginner, as a piano teacher and against the design brief.

## Verdict

The feature is quiet and well judged: nothing new shows until a reader has a favourite, the heart is the universal symbol in ink and never prints, the shelf reuses the style shelves' parts, and every screen state counted has exactly one red element. No blockers. Three things should be fixed before merge: on a phone the heart stands under the theme switch and adds a line to 30 % of titles (F1); Back after using the heart lands up to 127 px from the row the reader left (F2); and the pieces a beginner is likely to mark first are often the Songs shelf's samples, so the arrival page shows them twice (F3).

## What works

- **No favourites, nothing new.** Same DOM order (welcome, level pills, shelves), the same six `h2`, no Favourites node; the new CSS is scoped to `.title-row`, `.favourite-toggle` and `.favourites`. Checked from the DOM and the code, not by a pixel diff against `main`.
- **The heart.** A 24 px glyph with a 1.5 px outline in a 44 px round target, like the theme switch, centred on the title's first line within 0.5 px. It fills with ink in 120 ms, in both themes, never red. Named "Favourite" with `aria-pressed`; gray-1 hover disc, ink focus ring. Hidden in print; none on an unknown slug or (by code) an own file.
- **The shelf.** A style shelf's grammar without the description line: `Favourites  7 pieces`, the newest five, `All 7 favourites`. Expanding shows the rest in place, focuses the first new row and stays complete through a search and back. A search or an opened shelf takes it away; Escape brings it back.
- **Crowding.** With 1 or 3 favourites the phone's level pills sit at 354 and 466 px. With five or more the shelf is 346 px tall and the pills end at 657 px of 740, with no style-shelf row in view: acceptable as the owner's chosen trade for a returning reader.
- **One red.** Exactly one red root in every screen below: `Start with …` on the library, Play on the score, the heart off, hovered, focused or pressed. Print has none, by design.
- **Keyboard.** Score: Back, theme switch, the heading (focused on arrival), heart, credit link, the dock. Library: theme switch, Start, search, five favourite rows, `All 7 favourites`, the level pills. Space with the heading focused still starts Play; Tab then Space presses the heart.

## Screens looked at

Playwright's Chromium against the Vite dev server, at 1280 x 900 and 360 x 740, light and dark (confirmed from computed colours). Red roots counted by computed colour, background, border, outline, fill and stroke equal to `rgb(227, 0, 15)`.

- **Library:** 0, 1, 3 and 7 favourites, 7 expanded, a focused and a hovered row, search and Escape, the Songs shelf opened and closed, two favourites that are also samples, Back after the heart (first added, sixth added, last removed).
- **Score:** Mary Had a Little Lamb and the PDMX Sonatina en Sol major, each unpressed, hovered, pressed, focused and in print media; an unknown slug.
- **Title wrapping:** every title in `content/library-index.json` set in hidden clones of the score heading at 280 px (with the heart) and 328 px (without).

## Outcome

| #   | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| F1  | Fixed. The heart stands in the text flow after the title's last word, and the title row's `nowrap` holds the one break between heading and heart, so the heart takes the last word with it rather than standing alone on a line. At 360 px "Mary Had a Little Lamb" sets as "Mary Had a Little / Lamb ♡"; at 1280 px a one-line title stays on one line; the heart's middle is within 2 px of the line's. A last word too long for the line breaks (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), so none of the 3,228 titles runs past the column at 320 or 360 px (before, 2 did without the heart); 554 titles (17 %) take one more line at 360 px. |
| F2  | Fixed. The library remembers where its list stood (`offsetTop`) with the scroll offset and, coming back, moves the offset by as much as the list moved, before App scrolls (`useAnchoredLibraryScroll`); the top of the page stays the top. An e2e test adds and removes a favourite from a sample opened far down: the row returns within 3 px (126 px without the change).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| F3  | Declined. Leaving favourites out of the draw changes the shuffle's input, so the shelf the reader came from shows other samples the moment a heart is pressed, and Back no longer returns to the same rows (F2's rule). The repeated row is the smaller cost; `docs/app.md` records the reason.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| F4  | Fixed with F1: 0.5rem between the title and the heart's circle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
